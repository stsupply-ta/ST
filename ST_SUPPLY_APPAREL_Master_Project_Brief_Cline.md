# Part I — Business Profile

## 1. Identitas Bisnis

- **Nama brand:** ST SUPPLY APPAREL
- **Tagline resmi:** Online - Murah - Maksimal
- **Jenis bisnis:** Kombinasi produksi dan penjualan apparel
- **Cakupan produk:** Beragam kategori pakaian
- **Tujuan website:** Company profile, katalog produk, dan pengarah pembelian
- **Gaya visual:** Dark premium, dominan hitam-putih
- **Kanal:** WhatsApp, marketplace, media sosial, dan toko offline
- **Linktree resmi:** https://linktr.ee/st_supply
- **Lokasi yang diketahui:** Pakisaji, Tenggur, Kabupaten Tulungagung, Jawa Timur, Indonesia. Verifikasi alamat persis sebelum menampilkan peta atau petunjuk arah.

### Positioning

ST SUPPLY APPAREL diposisikan sebagai bisnis apparel yang menghubungkan kebutuhan produk pakaian dan layanan produksi dengan pengalaman pencarian serta pemesanan yang praktis melalui kanal online maupun offline.

Website perlu membedakan produk yang dijual, produk yang diproduksi sendiri, dan layanan produksi berdasarkan informasi operasional yang telah dikonfirmasi.

## 2. Company Overview

**ST SUPPLY APPAREL — Online - Murah - Maksimal**

ST SUPPLY APPAREL merupakan bisnis di bidang apparel yang menggabungkan kegiatan produksi dan penjualan produk pakaian. Dengan beragam kategori produk dan jalur pembelian yang terhubung secara digital, ST SUPPLY APPAREL berupaya memberikan kemudahan bagi pelanggan dalam menemukan produk yang sesuai dengan kebutuhan mereka.

Website resmi ST SUPPLY APPAREL berfungsi sebagai pusat informasi brand, katalog produk, dan akses menuju kanal pembelian. Pelanggan dapat mengenal produk, memperoleh informasi yang relevan, serta menghubungi tim ST SUPPLY APPAREL untuk pertanyaan dan kebutuhan pemesanan.

> **Catatan:** Ini adalah draf profil yang diusulkan. Pastikan semua pernyataan sesuai dengan kegiatan bisnis sebenarnya sebelum dipublikasikan.

## 3. Visi, Misi, dan Nilai Brand

### Visi

Menjadi bisnis apparel yang mudah dijangkau, dipercaya pelanggan, dan terus berkembang melalui kualitas layanan, pilihan produk, serta pemanfaatan teknologi digital.

### Misi

1. Mempermudah pelanggan menemukan produk apparel sesuai kebutuhan.
2. Menyediakan informasi produk dan layanan secara jelas dan transparan.
3. Menghubungkan kegiatan produksi dan penjualan dengan proses pemesanan yang praktis.
4. Mengembangkan pengalaman belanja melalui kanal digital dan offline.
5. Membangun hubungan jangka panjang dengan pelanggan melalui pelayanan yang responsif.

### Nilai Brand

- **Accessibility:** Memudahkan pelanggan mengakses informasi produk dan kanal pemesanan.
- **Value:** Menghadirkan penawaran yang kompetitif dengan informasi harga dan produk yang jelas.
- **Practicality:** Membuat proses pencarian produk dan komunikasi pemesanan lebih sederhana.
- **Adaptability:** Mendukung beragam kebutuhan apparel sesuai produk dan layanan yang benar-benar tersedia.
- **Trust:** Mengutamakan informasi yang transparan dan komunikasi yang jelas.

### Brand Promise

**Online. Murah. Maksimal.**

Sebuah komitmen brand untuk menghadirkan pengalaman mencari dan mendapatkan produk apparel yang mudah, bernilai, dan praktis.

## 4. Target Pelanggan

1. **Pelanggan individu:** Mencari pakaian untuk kebutuhan pribadi. Membutuhkan foto produk, kategori, ukuran, harga bila tersedia, dan tautan pembelian.
2. **Komunitas dan organisasi:** Berpotensi membutuhkan apparel untuk tim atau kegiatan, jika layanan tersebut tersedia.
3. **Pelanggan bisnis:** Reseller, bisnis, atau perusahaan yang membutuhkan produk apparel untuk dijual kembali atau untuk kebutuhan operasional, sesuai layanan dan kapasitas yang terkonfirmasi.

## 5. Arah Visual

### Palet Warna

| Nama | Kode | Penggunaan |
|---|---|---|
| Black | `#0B0B0B` | Hero, navigasi, footer, bidang utama |
| Charcoal | `#222222` | Panel gelap dan elemen sekunder |
| Off-white | `#F5F5F2` | Latar katalog dan area baca |
| Neutral gray | `#A3A3A3` | Teks sekunder dan garis |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Teks di latar gelap dan kontras |

Gunakan tipografi sans-serif modern, hierarki yang jelas, whitespace yang disengaja, grid rapi, dan foto produk autentik. Visual harus terasa premium tanpa menyampaikan kesan eksklusif atau mahal secara berlebihan, karena tagline brand menekankan harga terjangkau.

Hindari gradient berlebihan, glassmorphism berlebihan, neon, ornamen acak, animasi yang tidak diperlukan, klaim kemewahan, testimoni palsu, dan badge kepercayaan palsu.

## 6. Struktur Website yang Disarankan

- **Home:** Hero, tagline, CTA, produk unggulan, kategori, pengenalan brand, layanan terkonfirmasi, kanal pembelian, kontak, footer.
- **Catalog:** Grid produk, kategori, pencarian jika berguna, detail produk, dan tautan pembelian.
- **Product Detail:** Foto, nama, deskripsi, kategori, ukuran/warna/material/harga hanya jika datanya tersedia, serta tautan pembelian atau pertanyaan.
- **Production & Services:** Hanya menampilkan layanan produksi yang sudah dikonfirmasi.
- **About Us:** Profil bisnis, visi, misi, dan nilai brand.
- **Contact:** WhatsApp, marketplace, media sosial, Linktree, dan lokasi yang sudah diverifikasi.
- **404:** Halaman tidak ditemukan dengan tautan kembali ke Home dan Catalog.

Navigasi yang disarankan: `Home · Catalog · Production · About · Contact`

CTA yang disarankan:
- `Jelajahi Produk`
- `Lihat Detail`
- `Beli Produk`
- `Pesan via WhatsApp`
- `Konsultasikan Kebutuhan`
- `Hubungi Kami`

Tampilkan CTA hanya jika tujuan tautannya valid dan tindakannya benar-benar berfungsi.

## 7. Informasi yang Masih Perlu Dikonfirmasi

- Nomor WhatsApp resmi.
- URL resmi Shopee dan media sosial.
- Alamat toko yang persis dan URL peta.
- Logo resmi dan foto produk.
- Kategori produk yang benar-benar tersedia.
- Layanan produksi/custom yang benar-benar ditawarkan.
- Harga, ukuran, warna, material, SKU, stok, dan URL marketplace per produk jika relevan.
- Ketentuan pesanan, minimum order, waktu produksi, pengiriman, dan kebijakan lain bila ada.

Jangan mengarang informasi tersebut. Sembunyikan data yang belum tersedia atau tandai sebagai konfigurasi internal.

# Part II — Master Prompt untuk Cline

Salin prompt di bawah ini ke Cline setelah membuka folder proyek di VS Code.

## MASTER PROJECT BRIEF — ST SUPPLY APPAREL WEBSITE

### 1. Role

Act as a senior frontend engineer, UI/UX designer, accessibility specialist, and web performance engineer.

Build a complete, responsive, production-ready website for ST SUPPLY APPAREL.

The project combines:
- A professional company profile
- A multi-category apparel catalog
- Product detail views
- Production and service information
- Links to official purchase channels
- Direct WhatsApp inquiries and ordering

Do not build a generic fashion template. Create a distinctive, coherent digital presence that feels premium, practical, trustworthy, and product-focused.

### 2. Verified Project Configuration

- **Brand name:** ST SUPPLY APPAREL
- **Official tagline:** `Online - Murah - Maksimal`
- **Business model:** Combination of apparel production and product sales
- **Product scope:** Multiple apparel categories
- **Website purpose:** Company profile + product catalog + purchase redirection
- **Visual direction:** Premium dark monochrome, predominantly black and white
- **Available assets:** Some brand and product assets are available; inspect and reuse existing assets
- **WhatsApp:** The owner will provide the official number. Keep it configurable and never invent it
- **Official Linktree:** https://linktr.ee/st_supply
- **Location information currently available:** Pakisaji, Tenggur, Kabupaten Tulungagung, Jawa Timur, Indonesia. Verify the exact business address before embedding a map or publishing precise directions
- **Primary language:** Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia)

Target audience:
- Individual apparel customers
- Communities and organizations when relevant
- Business customers, resellers, or companies when relevant to actual services

### 3. Business Positioning

Position ST SUPPLY APPAREL as an apparel business connecting product sales and apparel production with convenient digital discovery and ordering.

Brand personality:
- Modern
- Professional
- Accessible
- Practical
- Confident
- Product-focused

Core brand message: `Online - Murah - Maksimal`

Suggested supporting copy:

> Temukan produk apparel yang sesuai dengan kebutuhanmu, jelajahi pilihan produk kami, dan hubungi ST SUPPLY APPAREL melalui kanal pemesanan resmi.

This is proposed marketing copy, not a claim about verified inventory or production capabilities.

Never invent:
- Company founding year
- Legal status
- Certifications
- Production capacity
- Factory size
- Customer counts
- Client names
- Testimonials
- Product inventory
- Prices
- Material specifications
- Shipping policies
- Warranty policies
- Bulk-order minimums
- Production lead times

Represent missing facts as configurable fields or omit them from public-facing content.

### 4. Design System

Create a premium monochrome visual identity.

Base colors:
- Black: `#0B0B0B`
- Charcoal: `#222222`
- Off-white: `#F5F5F2`
- Neutral gray: `#A3A3A3`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

Use black and charcoal for hero sections, navigation, strong visual blocks, and footer. Use off-white or white surfaces for product browsing, long-form reading, and product information.

Use restrained borders, strong typography, deliberate whitespace, clean grid alignment, and consistent spacing.

Avoid:
- Excessive gradients
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Unnecessary neon colors
- Generic template sections
- Excessive rounded cards
- Random decorative shapes
- Unnecessary animation
- Fake luxury claims
- Fake reviews or trust badges

The design must balance a premium presentation with the brand's accessible pricing message.

#### Typography

Choose a highly readable modern sans-serif family.

Use a clear type scale:
- Strong editorial hero headline
- Medium-weight section headings
- Readable body copy
- Compact metadata and category labels

Avoid overly decorative fonts and excessive letter spacing in long text.

#### Motion

Use subtle transitions only when they improve feedback or navigation. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Avoid animation that delays access to content or reduces mobile performance.

### 5. Responsive Experience

Design mobile-first and support small mobile screens, large mobile screens, tablets, desktop, and wide desktop layouts.

Requirements:
- No horizontal overflow
- Comfortable touch targets
- Readable typography
- Responsive product grids
- Mobile navigation with an accessible menu button
- Clear focus states
- Sticky navigation only when appropriate
- Purchase actions easy to locate without obstructing content

Do not simply shrink the desktop design. Reorganize content based on screen size.

### 6. Required Site Architecture

#### A. Home Page

Include:
1. Responsive header and navigation.
2. Brand-led hero section.
3. Official tagline.
4. Primary CTA: `Jelajahi Produk`.
5. Secondary CTA: `Hubungi Kami`.
6. Featured products based on actual product data.
7. Category navigation.
8. Brand introduction.
9. Production and service overview, only for confirmed services.
10. Purchase-channel section.
11. Contact and location section.
12. Footer with verified links.

The hero must immediately communicate who the brand is, what type of business it operates, and where visitors can explore products or make inquiries.

Do not use generic claims such as "Indonesia's number one apparel brand."

#### B. Product Catalog

Create a reusable catalog experience.

Required functionality:
- Product grid
- Category filtering based on available data
- Product cards with consistent image ratios
- Product name
- Category
- Short description
- Price only when verified
- Product detail or purchase CTA
- Graceful image fallbacks
- Empty search/filter state
- Responsive behavior

Only render filters when they correspond to real categories in the product dataset.

If search is implemented, search across product name, category, and description.

Do not add sorting, filters, or controls that do not work.

#### C. Product Detail

Provide a dedicated product page or accessible detail view.

Potential fields:
- Product images
- Product name
- Category
- Description
- Available sizes
- Available colors
- Material
- Price
- Product SKU
- Marketplace URL
- WhatsApp inquiry action

Each optional field must be omitted when unavailable.

Do not fabricate sizes, colors, material specifications, prices, or stock availability.

When a field is missing, show a neutral message or provide a product inquiry CTA rather than a fictional value.

When a verified marketplace URL exists, direct customers to it.

When no marketplace URL exists but WhatsApp is configured, generate a prefilled WhatsApp message containing the product name.

#### D. Production & Services

Create a section explaining production or custom apparel services only to the extent that the business owner confirms those services.

Possible service categories may be prepared as configurable data, not as automatic factual claims:
- Custom apparel
- Bulk or organization orders
- Apparel production
- Product sourcing or supply

Do not publish an item as an active service until confirmed.

If details such as MOQ, available printing methods, production capacity, or lead times are unavailable, do not display made-up values.

Provide a configurable inquiry CTA for prospective customers.

#### E. About Page

Include:
- Company overview
- Brand vision
- Brand mission
- Brand values
- Optional business history, only when verified

Use the following proposed vision as editable copy:

> Menjadi bisnis apparel yang mudah dijangkau, dipercaya pelanggan, dan terus berkembang melalui kualitas layanan, pilihan produk, serta pemanfaatan teknologi digital.

Use the following proposed mission as editable copy:
1. Mempermudah pelanggan menemukan produk apparel sesuai kebutuhan.
2. Menyediakan informasi produk dan layanan secara jelas dan transparan.
3. Menghubungkan kegiatan produksi dan penjualan dengan proses pemesanan yang praktis.
4. Mengembangkan pengalaman belanja melalui kanal digital dan offline.
5. Membangun hubungan jangka panjang dengan pelanggan melalui pelayanan yang responsif.

#### F. Contact Page

Include configurable fields for:

- Map URL

Only show active channels when their destinations are verified.

Use `https://linktr.ee/st_supply` as a general channel-discovery fallback where appropriate.

Do not invent phone numbers or social media handles.

Do not publish a map pin based on an approximate address.

#### G. Footer

Include:
- Brand name
- Official tagline
- Main navigation
- Verified purchase channels

#### H. Not Found Page

If a routing solution is used, provide a useful 404 page with a link back to the homepage and catalog.

### 7. Content and Conversion Strategy

Write natural, concise Indonesian copy.

Avoid:
- Excessive English marketing jargon
- Unsupported superlatives
- Fake urgency
- Fake discounts
- Fake customer testimonials
- Fake stock counters
- Fake trust metrics

Use consistent CTA labels:
- `Jelajahi Produk`
- `Lihat Detail`
- `Beli Produk`
- `Pesan via WhatsApp`
- `Konsultasikan Kebutuhan`
- `Hubungi Kami`

Use `Pesan via WhatsApp` only when a valid WhatsApp number is configured.

Use `Beli Produk` only when there is a valid purchase destination. Otherwise use an inquiry CTA.

#### WhatsApp Integration

Create a configurable contact object containing:
- `phoneNumber`
- `defaultMessage`
- `enabled`

The phone number must initially be an empty string or a clear configuration placeholder.

Normalize the number safely for WhatsApp's international format. Encode message parameters with the appropriate URL-encoding function.

For product-specific inquiries, include the actual product name in the message.

Do not construct WhatsApp links from an empty or invalid number.

If WhatsApp is not configured, hide the WhatsApp CTA and show the verified Linktree or another active channel instead.

Do not expose private credentials.

### 8. Product Data Architecture

Keep product information separate from presentation components.

Create a central product data source.

Suggested product fields:
- `id`
- `slug`
- `name`
- `category`
- `description`
- `image`
- `gallery` (optional)
- `price` (optional)
- `currency` (optional)
- `sizes` (optional)
- `colors` (optional)
- `material` (optional)
- `sku` (optional)
- `marketplaceUrl` (optional)
- `whatsappEnabled` (optional)
- `featured`
- `published`

Rules:
- Only published real products appear in public-facing sections.
- Never generate fake production inventory.
- Never display placeholder prices.
- Never claim live stock availability without a real data source.
- Keep image paths local and easy to replace.
- Validate required fields where practical.
- Avoid duplicating product content in multiple components.

Use a clear example schema and a documented empty catalog state when actual product data is not yet available.

If placeholder records are necessary for layout development, clearly mark them as development fixtures and ensure they cannot be mistaken for actual inventory in production.

### 9. Asset Management

Inspect existing assets before creating or replacing files.

Look for:
- Brand logo
- Product photography
- Apparel mockups
- Production images
- Store images
- Existing fonts and icons
- Existing website design references

Preserve useful existing files. Create a simple asset organization structure if needed.

Suggested organization:
- `public/images/brand/`
- `public/images/products/`
- `public/images/services/`
- `public/images/general/`

Do not overwrite original assets.

Do not assume that a random online image is an official product photo.

Use supplied genuine product images whenever possible.

If an asset is missing, use a neutral placeholder during development and document the replacement requirement.

Avoid downloading copyrighted imagery without appropriate rights.

All interactive elements must work with mouse, keyboard, and touch where applicable.

Do not use clickable `div` elements as substitutes for proper links and buttons.

### 11. SEO and Social Sharing

Implement:
- Descriptive page titles
- Meta descriptions
- Canonical URLs only when the production domain is known
- Open Graph metadata
- Theme color
- Favicon
- Meaningful image alt text
- Semantic heading structure
- Internal links
- Descriptive route names
- Product-specific metadata where appropriate

Do not invent the official domain.

Keep domain-specific configuration easy to update after deployment.

Do not add Product structured data containing invented prices, ratings, inventory, or brand information.

Only implement structured data for fields that are actually known.

### 12. Performance

Prioritize:
- Appropriate image sizing
- Responsive images where practical
- Modern image formats when available
- Lazy loading for below-the-fold images
- Eager loading only for the main hero image when appropriate
- Explicit image dimensions or aspect ratios
- Minimal JavaScript
- Avoiding unnecessary layout shifts
- Lightweight transitions
- Efficient component rendering

Do not lazy-load the primary hero image if it harms the initial visual loading experience.

### 13. Accessibility

Implement:
- Sufficient text contrast
- Keyboard navigation
- Visible focus indicators
- Descriptive link labels
- Accessible mobile navigation
- Form labels when forms exist
- Appropriate alt text
- Reduced-motion support
- Proper heading order

Do not use color alone to communicate important product or interaction states.

### 14. Testing and Quality Assurance

Use available project scripts to:
- Run linting
- Run type checks if configured
- Build the production bundle
- Check broken internal links where tooling is available
- Check missing asset paths
- Check responsive behavior
- Check for nonfunctional buttons
- Check browser console errors when browser tooling is available

Fix errors caused by the implementation.

Do not claim tests passed unless they were actually run.

Do not install unrelated packages simply to satisfy an unnecessary tool preference.

### 15. Workflow

**Phase 1 — Repository Audit**
Inspect files, dependencies, assets, routes, and current implementation.

**Phase 2 — Implementation Plan**
Provide a concise plan identifying:
- Existing architecture to preserve
- Pages and components required
- Assets available
- Missing information requiring owner input

**Phase 3 — Design System**
Establish colors, typography, spacing, breakpoints, and reusable component conventions.

**Phase 4 — Implementation**
Build the actual pages and working interactions incrementally.

**Phase 5 — Integration**
Connect the central product data source, configured contact details, verified external links, and available assets.

**Phase 6 — QA**
Run available checks, fix errors, and review mobile and desktop behavior.

**Phase 7 — Documentation**
Update `README.md` with:
- Project overview
- Requirements
- Installation
- Development
- Build
- Asset replacement
- Product data editing
- WhatsApp configuration
- Social link configuration
- Deployment instructions

Do not stop after writing a plan. After inspecting the repository and presenting the plan, proceed with implementation unless a genuinely blocking decision requires owner input.

### 16. Definition of Done

The website is complete when:
- The production build succeeds.
- The main pages and navigation work.
- The dark monochrome design is consistent.
- The website is responsive across mobile and desktop.
- The catalog uses centralized product data.
- Product links and contact links work correctly.
- Empty or missing data is handled gracefully.
- No fabricated business claims are displayed.
- No fake product prices or stock values are displayed.
- No WhatsApp link is generated from an empty number.
- Accessibility basics are implemented.
- SEO metadata is present and accurate.
- README documentation is updated.
- Any unverified business details are clearly listed for owner review.

Build a cohesive, professional first version. Prefer maintainable code, authentic content, and reliable functionality over unnecessary complexity.
